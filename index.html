About the Project

What is TerraSagip?

Landslides remain one of the most destructive geological hazards in the Philippines, especially in Eastern Visayas where steep terrain and heavy rainfall create high-risk conditions. Conventional monitoring methods are often expensive, labor-intensive, and provide only periodic observations.

TerraSagip (from terra = earth + Filipino sagip = to save/rescue) is a low-cost ESP32-based Internet of Things prototype designed to continuously monitor key environmental and ground-condition parameters associated with slope instability.

The system integrates multiple sensors, processes data onboard, transmits readings via LoRaWAN to a web monitoring platform, and automatically sends SMS alerts when predefined risk thresholds are reached — supporting community preparedness and early warning.

System Capabilities

Key Features

TerraSagip combines environmental sensing, edge processing, long-range communication, and automated alerts into one prototype.

💧

Soil Moisture Sensing

Capacitive analog sensor continuously tracks soil water content — a critical factor in slope stability.

🌧️

Rainfall Intensity

Rain/raindrop sensor detects precipitation levels that can trigger or accelerate landslide conditions.

📐

Slope Inclination

MPU6050 accelerometer/gyro monitors tilt and orientation changes in the monitoring setup.

📳

Ground Vibration

SW-420 vibration sensor detects oscillatory movement that may indicate instability.

🌡️

Temp & Humidity

DHT22 provides supplementary environmental context for the monitoring environment.

🛰️

GPS Positioning

NEO-6M module reports the exact geographical location of the monitoring unit.

📡

LoRaWAN Transmission

Low-power, long-range wireless data transfer to a web-based monitoring platform.

📱

SMS Early Warning

Automated text alerts sent to designated recipients when risk thresholds are exceeded.

System Flow

How TerraSagip Works

From sensing to alerting — a simple four-stage pipeline.

1

Sense

Sensors collect soil moisture, rainfall, tilt, vibration, temperature, humidity, and GPS data.

2

Process

ESP32 processes readings and compares them against predefined risk thresholds.

3

Transmit

Data is sent via LoRaWAN to a web monitoring platform for real-time viewing.

4

Alert

When thresholds are reached, visual indicators, buzzer, and SMS notifications activate.

Research Goals

Objectives

General objective: Develop and evaluate TerraSagip in terms of sensor monitoring, data transmission, and warning notification performance.

1

Develop TerraSagip by integrating the ESP32, selected sensors, LoRaWAN, GPS location reporting, and SMS-based warning notifications.

2

Establish multi-sensor monitoring for accurate soil moisture, rainfall, vibration, slope inclination, temperature, humidity, and GPS data.

3

Create an automated detection and warning mechanism that analyzes real-time data, classifies risk levels, and generates SMS alerts.

4

Evaluate functionality, accuracy, responsiveness, LoRaWAN reliability, SMS delivery, usability, and overall performance.

5

Compare TerraSagip with conventional ground-instability monitoring methods in frequency, response time, reliability, and notification delivery.

Impact

Residents in landslide-prone areas receive timely SMS notifications that can support awareness and preparedness.

Local government and disaster offices gain a reference for low-cost sensor-based monitoring tools.

🎓 Schools & Students

🔬 Researchers

Provides a foundation for future work on multi-sensor systems, LoRaWAN performance, and community early warning.
